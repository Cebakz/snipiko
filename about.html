About & contact

Snipiko is an independent number-and-rectangle puzzle. Each clue asks you to draw a rectangle with the same number of cells. Fill rows and columns, clear space and plan your next move.

Game modes

Classic is the main mode: an open-ended score challenge built around finding useful rectangles and clearing space.

Journey Beta offers individual levels, objectives and move limits. Blitz Beta adds clues over active play time, with a visible arrival countdown. A guided tutorial introduces the shared controls and scoring rules.

Play on your phone or computer

The game runs in a web browser with JavaScript enabled. Use touch or a mouse to draw rectangles. Keyboard controls are also available. There is no download or account required for the basic game.

Support and privacy contact

For questions, a problem with the game, or requests about your data, contact the Snipiko operator at cebakzgames@gmail.com. Include your browser and the steps that led to a problem. Do not send passwords, identity documents or bank details.
