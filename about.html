SNITRIX / ABOUT THE GAME

Small rules. Room to think.

Snitrix is an independent number-and-rectangle puzzle for the browser. One clue defines an area; the player decides which rectangle makes the best use of the board.

A shape puzzle that keeps moving

The game combines a simple area rule with an ongoing space-management problem. Rectangles turn into blocks; complete rows and columns clear those blocks; incoming clues change the next decision. The choice is not just whether a rectangle fits, but whether it leaves room to keep playing.

Classic is the main mode: an open-ended personal score challenge without a move timer. Journey adds level goals and move limits. Blitz is a five-minute score challenge opened from Home, with clues refilled after valid moves. Daily Challenge offers a dated goal and move budget. Journey and Blitz are marked Beta because these modes are still evolving.

The illustrated guide explains valid and invalid rectangles. The strategy page goes further into useful empty space, planning clears and scoring. Both are written specifically for Snitrix.

Play in the browser you already use

Snitrix runs with JavaScript enabled on a phone, tablet or computer. Draw with touch or a mouse; keyboard controls are available too. Basic play needs no download, payment or account.

Progress is saved locally in the browser when storage is available. An optional online ranking is separate from the basic game. Local progress does not automatically follow you to a different device, browser or private window.

Real advertising is currently disabled. Optional advertising features remain subject to availability and consent. For details about browser storage, analytics and optional online features, read the privacy policy.

Contact & support

A question, bug or privacy request?

Contact the Snitrix operator at cebakzgames@gmail.com.

For a game problem, include the mode, the device and browser you used, what you tried to do and what happened instead. If you attach a screenshot, remove private information first.

For a privacy request, describe what you want to access, correct or delete. Never send a password, ranking token, bank details or identity documents with an ordinary support message.

If your saved game seems missing

Check that you opened the same browser and site address as before. Try a normal reload and return to Home. Avoid clearing site data while investigating: that removes progress stored on your device. If the problem persists, contact us with the steps above.

If the puzzle is unclear

Begin with the in-game tutorial or the rectangle examples. You can take your time in Classic. The five-point move checklist can help you identify what makes a selection invalid.
