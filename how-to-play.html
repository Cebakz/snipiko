How to play

Snipiko is played on a 6 × 6 board. Your goal is to turn numbered clues into rectangles and clear full rows and columns.

  1. Choose one clue. Drag across a rectangle containing exactly one number. Its area must match that number: a 6 fits a 2 × 3 rectangle or a 1 × 6 rectangle.
  2. Use available cells. A rectangle cannot overlap an occupied cell or contain a second clue. Tap a clue numbered 1 to fill its single cell.
  3. Clear lines. Completing a row or column removes its filled cells. Completing several lines together earns extra points.
  4. Keep the combo. A line clear increases your combo. You may make one move without clearing a line and preserve it; two consecutive moves without a clear reset the combo.
  5. Watch the next numbers. The queue shows the next three values, but their future positions are not shown. Keep useful empty areas for them.

Classic: the main mode

New clues arrive after valid moves. Classic ends when no legal rectangle fits the clues on the board. Keep clearing space and beat your personal best.

Journey Beta

Each level has a move limit and specific goals shown above the board. Complete those goals to win; finish with moves to spare to earn more stars.

Blitz Beta: timed clues

New clues arrive as active play time passes. Playing a rectangle does not immediately replace its clue. Watch NEXT and the arrival countdown. When there are temporarily no legal rectangles, wait for the next clue; waiting by itself is not a defeat.

Keep room for incoming clues and watch the visible clue limit. Blitz can end when no rectangle fits and the next clue has no room, or when the make-space countdown expires because an arrival remains blocked or the clue limit is full. Menus, Home and a hidden tab pause the clock. There is no fixed two-minute round.

Hints and optional rewards

Each new Classic game starts with three hints. A hint highlights a recommended move without changing the board. Viewing the same highlighted hint again does not consume another credit. Where advertising is available, completing an optional ad can provide an extra hint or one continuation after a Classic defeat. An incomplete ad does not grant that reward. Test versions may offer free benefits or clearly labelled mock ads.

Controls and saved progress

On a phone, press and drag with one finger, then release. With a keyboard, use arrow keys to move, Enter or Space to begin or confirm a rectangle, and Escape to cancel the selection. Progress and preferences are saved in this browser when storage is available.
