How to play Snitrix

One number tells you the area. You choose the shape. Learn the rules on the same 6 × 6 board you will use in the game.

Start with one rectangle

A clue is a numbered square. Drag from one corner of a rectangle to the opposite corner, then release. A valid rectangle contains exactly one clue, covers only available squares and has the same area as its clue. Width multiplied by height gives the area.

You can rotate a shape. A 6 also fits a 2 × 3, 1 × 6 or 6 × 1 rectangle when the board has room. A 4 can be 2 × 2, 1 × 4 or 4 × 1. A clue 1 needs only its own square: tap it to fill that square.

Why a selection may be rejected

The right area is only part of the rule. Another clue inside your rectangle makes it invalid, even when that clue is small. You also cannot cover an already filled block or stretch a rectangle outside the board.

Make space by clearing lines

Accepted rectangles become filled blocks. Complete all six squares in a row or column to clear that line and open room for later clues. A single move can complete more than one line, including a row and a column at the same time.

Look at what the board will be like after the clear, not just how neatly a rectangle fits now. Leaving a hole that cannot fit a remaining clue can be more costly than missing a large clear.

In Classic, clearing all filled blocks earns an All Clear bonus of 1,500 points. Multiple-line clears also score more than single-line clears. See worked scoring examples.

Build a combo

A line clear increases the combo counter. Normally, you can make one move without a clear while keeping the combo; a second consecutive move without a clear resets it. Another clear resets that one-move allowance.

Classic also protects a combo after an All Clear until the next line clear. Follow the combo indicator in the game: it reflects the current state. Classic has no move timer, so take time to check your options.

Think one move ahead.

Use the non-clearing move to prepare a line, rather than filling space simply because a rectangle fits.

Choose your mode

Classic — start here

Classic is the main, open-ended score mode. New clues arrive after valid moves. Clear space, plan around the visible NEXT queue and try to improve your own best. A game ends when no legal rectangle fits the remaining clues. The board and queue matter more than speed.

Journey Beta

Journey uses individual levels with objectives and move limits. Read the goal shown above the board: finishing a level is different from chasing an unlimited Classic score. Levels can introduce obstacles, so use the instructions shown for that level.

Blitz: 5 Minutes Beta

5 Minutes is the Blitz game opened from Home. Score as many points as you can against five minutes of active play. Clues refill after valid moves, as in Classic. Moves in the final 30 seconds earn double points. If no move remains, the game can clear blocks to make room at a cost of 10 seconds; with too little time left, the match ends.

Home, menus and a hidden tab pause the active-play clock.

Daily Challenge

Open Daily Challenge from Home for a dated objective and a limited number of moves. Read the goal and move budget shown before you start: a score target and a line-clear target need different plans. Daily progress and streaks are stored in this browser; return on the same device and browser to keep them.

Hints and optional continuation

Use the Hint control when it is available to highlight a recommended rectangle without changing the board. Availability is shown by the game. Viewing the same highlighted hint again does not consume another credit. Optional extra help or a continuation may be offered according to availability. A continuation keeps the current game and score while removing some blocks.

Real advertising is currently disabled. There is no requirement to watch an advertisement to start the basic game.

Common questions

Why can’t I draw the shape I want?

Check its area, check for a second clue, and check for filled blocks. Changing orientation often helps: a 6 can fit as 3 × 2 or 2 × 3. See the two examples above.

Do I have to use the next number immediately?

NEXT previews incoming values, not future positions. Work with the clues already on the board and keep useful space for the numbers that are coming.

Can I play without sound or without an account?

Yes. The rules are visible on the board, and sound can be adjusted in the game settings. Basic play and a local personal best do not require a ranking account.

How do I report a problem?

Email cebakzgames@gmail.com with the mode, device, browser and steps that caused it. A screenshot helps if it contains no private information. Do not send passwords, player tokens or identity documents.

This guide describes the published browser game. Beta modes can change; objectives and prompts shown in the game take priority for the current level.
