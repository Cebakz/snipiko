Privacy policy

Storage, online features and the choices available to you.

Updated November 25, 2026
Your privacyContact the operatorSaved game help

This policy describes data used by the Snitrix browser game and its supporting pages. For privacy requests, contact Cebakz Games at cebakzgames@gmail.com.

Progress stored on your device

The game uses browser storage for your saved Classic board and scores, Journey progress, Blitz board/queue/remaining time, tutorial completion, settings, and recent gameplay/ad events. These records allow you to resume and prevent repeated rewards. Browser storage remains until you clear it, overwrite it through play, or your browser removes it. Clearing site data deletes local progress and preferences.

Cloud saves and recovery codes

When the game has access to browser storage and a cloud service is configured, it can create a save and automatically send a copy as progress changes. The copy can contain mode progress, scores, tutorial and settings data, and saved ranking profile or score records. Large entries may be omitted. The service uses a browser-held credential and displays a separate recovery code in Settings. Anyone with the recovery code can attempt to recover that save, so keep it private. Applying a recovered copy can overwrite matching progress on the receiving browser.

Cloud saving and recovery need an internet connection and a responding service. Contact us to ask about access or deletion of a stored cloud save. Do not include your recovery code in a routine support message.

Usage statistics

On the public game, usage analytics is on by default unless the browser signals Do Not Track or you previously switched it off. When enabled, the game sends gameplay events, browser or session identifiers, device/browser information and interaction timing to PostHog. Session recording and automatic capture of clicks or form activity are disabled. To switch game analytics off, use Usage analytics in Settings. The landing page, guides and Rectangle Workshop do not run analytics scripts. Workshop choices stay in the open page and are discarded when you leave. Analytics requests are transmitted over the internet and processed by the provider; its infrastructure may be outside your country. See PostHog's privacy policy. Contact us about access or deletion of statistics associated with your browser identifier.

Advertising

Real advertising is currently disabled on this deployment. Clearly labelled test pages may load Google's official mock advertisements, which do not generate revenue.

When Google advertising is enabled, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads based on previous visits to this or other websites. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalized or not personalized. See how Google uses data from partner sites and Google's privacy policy.

You can manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center and participating third-party preferences at AdChoices. Consent choices depend on region and on the consent provider being available. If the game shows an Ad privacy control, it can open the available privacy options. Rejecting or blocking ads can make optional ad rewards unavailable; the basic game remains playable.

Online ranking

If you use the online ranking when the service is available, it stores your chosen nickname, player code, friend links and submitted scores. Your nickname and score may appear on leaderboards. A locally stored token identifies your player; do not share that token. Contact us to request removal of ranking data.

Hosting and contact messages

Hosting and advertising services receive technical information such as IP addresses and browser request details when serving the site. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to handle your request. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Your choices and requests

You can clear local site data, adjust available analytics and advertising choices, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data we hold, subject to applicable requirements. Browser settings and clearing site data do not automatically delete information already sent to service providers. If data practices change, this page will be updated.
