Privacy policy

This policy describes data used by the Snitrix browser game. Contact the operator at cebakzgames@gmail.com for privacy requests.

Progress stored on your device

The game uses browser storage for your saved Classic board and scores, Journey progress, Blitz board/queue/remaining time, tutorial completion, settings, and recent gameplay/ad events. These records allow you to resume and prevent repeated rewards. Browser storage remains until you clear it, overwrite it through play, or your browser removes it. Clearing site data deletes local progress and preferences.

Usage statistics

On deployments where usage analytics is enabled, the game sends gameplay events, session identifiers, device/browser information and interaction timing to PostHog. It does not enable session recordings or capture typed form content. You can disable usage analytics in Settings where that option is shown. Analytics requests are transmitted over the internet and processed by the provider; its infrastructure may be outside your country. See PostHog's privacy policy. Contact us about access or deletion of statistics associated with your browser identifier.

Advertising

Real advertising is currently disabled on this deployment. Clearly labelled test pages may load Google's official mock advertisements, which do not generate revenue.

When Google advertising is enabled, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads based on previous visits to this or other websites. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalized or not personalized. See how Google uses data from partner sites and Google's privacy policy.

You can manage Google ad personalization in My Ad Center and participating third-party preferences at AdChoices. Where a consent message is required, the configured consent provider presents the available choices. The game's Ad privacy control reopens Google's options when available. Rejecting or blocking ads can make optional ad rewards unavailable; the basic game remains playable.

Online ranking

The online ranking stores your chosen nickname, player code, friend links and submitted scores. Your nickname and score may appear on leaderboards. A locally stored token identifies your player; do not share that token. Entries remain on the ranking server until removed by the operator. Contact us to request deletion.

Hosting and contact messages

Hosting and advertising services receive technical information such as IP addresses and browser request details when serving the site. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to handle your request. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support.

Your choices and requests

You can clear local site data, adjust available analytics and advertising choices, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data we hold, subject to applicable requirements. Browser settings and clearing site data do not automatically delete information already sent to service providers. If data practices change, this page will be updated.
